## Evidence to prepare for the panel

Each member should be ready to show or discuss at least one artifact from their
area:

- A feature, migration, test, document, issue, or commit they contributed to.
- The requirement or user problem it addresses.
- How the frontend, database, and security rules interact in that flow.
- A test case they performed, including the expected and actual result.
- One limitation or next improvement they understand.

Contribution wording should remain factual. Git history, screenshots, test
results, research notes, and presentation demonstrations are stronger evidence
than broad claims without an artifact.
