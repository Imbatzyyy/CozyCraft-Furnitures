# Evidence required before compliance sign-off

September 9, 2026. Code improvements are not a legal certification.

- Confirm the legal seller name, approved public business address, registration details, and customer-care hours. Enter only verified information in Store Settings.
- Confirm refund processing times, COD refund arrangements, change-of-mind eligibility, and return-shipping costs. The public policy does not invent these commitments.
- Supply an asset register: asset URL/path, creator, original source, licence/permission evidence, and any model/property releases. An Unsplash notice does not license unrelated supplier photos.
- Identify test orders/reviews with evidence before removing any production content. Preserve genuine critical feedback. No reviews were deleted by this release.
- Approve a record-specific retention schedule and operational deletion process. Do not publish unverified deletion deadlines.
- Review whether gender/date of birth are needed at all; they are optional in the web form. Existing records were preserved rather than erased without a scoped data decision.
- Complete authenticated keyboard/screen-reader testing of checkout, OTP, returns, uploads, and all admin workflows. Automated public-page checks are not complete WCAG certification.
- Review actual service coverage, assembly offerings, product specifications, and supplier authorizations before approving claims.

Public privacy and terms routes now use the versioned source policy instead of potentially stale managed-page bodies. Changes to those source policies should be reviewed together with the signup policy version. Refund request windows use existing shared Store Settings; no additional polling is introduced.
